## Licence tiers

| Tier | Organisation size | Annual |
|---|---|---|
| Startup | under 50 employees | $18,000 |
| SME | 50–500 | $75,000 |
| Enterprise | 500–5,000 | $350,000 |
| Large enterprise | 5,000–50,000 | $1,200,000 |
| Strategic / government | 50,000+ or national | $5,000,000 |

## What each tier adds

Deployment nodes, compliance documentation, quarterly audit packages, custom
fine-tuning, multi-region support, SLA, and for the strategic tier a full
sovereign deployment with regulatory co-filing.
